How it works

Three steps from a decade of IMD records
to an advisory people can act on.

  1. 01

    Observe

    IMD Pune's 1° gridded daily maximum temperature, 2014–2024. Each city is the grid cell it sits in: 40,180 real readings, no synthetic data.

  2. 02

    Forecast

    Seven ridge-regression models, one for each day ahead, learn from the last week, the season and the climatological normal. Trained on 2014–22, tuned on 2023, tested on 2024.

  3. 03

    Warn

    IMD's heatwave criteria are applied to each forecast plus a safety margin, because a missed heatwave costs more than a false alarm. Days 5–7 are flagged low-confidence.
